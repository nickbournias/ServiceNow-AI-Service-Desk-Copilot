# VPN Connectivity Issues

Symptoms:
- User cannot connect to the corporate VPN
- VPN client reports connection failed or timed out
- User connects but cannot access internal resources
- VPN connection repeatedly disconnects

Suggested investigation:
- Verify the user's internet connection
- Confirm VPN credentials are valid
- Check VPN client configuration and version
- Verify the VPN service is operational
- Determine whether the issue affects multiple users
